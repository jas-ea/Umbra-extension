# Product Contract

## One job

Umbra keeps the part of a webpage in use clear and darkens everything around it.

## Preserve the page

Umbra draws a visual layer over the existing page. The website keeps its layout, content, controls, and native pointer behavior.

## Confirm before switching

The first automatic focus requires a stable dwell or a settled scroll. When the pointer leaves a section, its cutout retires quickly. The next section appears after one candidate completes the switching delay. Returning to the previous section during a short grace period can restore it immediately.

The overlay must never flash as a full-page dimmer between adjacent items. An uncertain handoff shows no cutout until the next candidate is confirmed.

## Match the task scale

Articles, opened messages, posts, answers, and comparable detail regions can be individual targets. Mail inboxes, calendars, grids, and conversations are collection targets, so people can scan them without isolating one row or cell.

Menus, submenus, popovers, dialogs, dragging, and fullscreen suspend the overlay. Editing and user-started media are explicit interaction states and outrank pointer inference.

When automatic evidence is weak, Umbra waits for a direct selection.

## Keep daily controls small

The popup exposes one main action, page darkness, site mode, tab pause, and Settings. The Settings page contains only choices that apply across sites. Engine timing and geometry remain product defaults.

## Stay local

Selection and geometry run inside the extension. Umbra stores preferences through Chrome storage and sends no page content or behavior analytics to Cassini Research.

## Ship only what is true

Every visible control must affect production behavior. Public copy must not claim attention detection, comprehension gains, productivity gains, accessibility outcomes, or universal site support without evidence.
